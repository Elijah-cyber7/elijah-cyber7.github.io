Projects

NBFM · DSP · STT

Warden

A real-time software-defined radio pipeline on a HackRF One. It receives NBFM push-to-talk, demodulates to audio, and streams speech-to-text — all in Python, with no GNU Radio runtime. The DSP chain is hand-written: a 1024-tap Kaiser FIR, conjugate-multiply FM demodulation, multi-stage decimation, RMS squelch, and Goertzel CTCSS gating, feeding Vosk into a callsign-triggered dispatch layer.
